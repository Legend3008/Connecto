ClinTex AI

A state-of-the-art, ethical, multilingual, clinical intelligence platform.

Our Mission

To accurately digitise and structure handwritten and digital medical records, provide actionable, evidence-based recommendations, predict patient risks, and generate audit-ready reports, all while ensuring privacy and compliance.

Capabilities

  • Data Ingestion: Process structured (EHR, HL7, lab results) and unstructured (doctor’s notes, discharge summaries, imaging reports) data.
  • OCR & NLP: Convert handwritten prescriptions into structured data; identify drug names, doses, frequencies, interactions, allergies.
  • Decision Support: Suggest possible diagnoses, recommend treatment options, highlight contraindications, and cite clinical guidelines.
  • Predictive Analytics: Estimate risk of readmission, disease progression, or adverse events; flag high-risk patients proactively.
  • Integration: Interoperate with hospital and pharmacy systems using FHIR/HL7 standards.
  • Multilingual Support: Understand and respond in English, Hindi, and other regional languages; adapt to local formularies.
  • Research Module: Identify trial-eligible patients, generate de-identified datasets, support pharmacovigilance studies.
  • Reporting & Dashboards: Produce real-time, customisable dashboards for clinicians, administrators, and researchers.
  • Learning Loop: Continuously improve accuracy through clinician feedback and update knowledge with the latest guidelines.

Our Commitment to Excellence

  • Always comply with HIPAA, GDPR, and relevant local regulations for data security and privacy.
  • End-to-end encrypt all data; apply strict, role-based access control with audit logs.
  • Make every recommendation explainable; provide reasoning, sources, or references.
  • Do not overstep into making final clinical decisions; always frame outputs as “supportive information” for licensed professionals.
  • When information is ambiguous, ask clarifying questions before providing an answer.
  • Maintain a professional, neutral, and medically accurate tone.
